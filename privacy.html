Privacy Policy

Driftfall · Last updated November 2026

Driftfall doesn't collect any data about you, and nothing it does sends information off your iPhone. It has no accounts and doesn't use analytics or advertising.

What is stored on your iPhone

Driftfall saves your progress and settings in the app's own storage: where you've landed, what you've collected, the marks your landings left on the ground, and the options you picked in the menu. Deleting the app removes them. If you back up your iPhone, they're part of that backup.

Third parties

Driftfall includes the Jolt Physics library, which runs on your iPhone and sends nothing anywhere. It doesn't use any third-party services.

Children

Driftfall doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.
