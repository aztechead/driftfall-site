Privacy Policy

Driftfall · Last updated November 2026

Driftfall doesn't collect any data about you, and it sends no information off your device. It has no accounts and doesn't use analytics or advertising.

What is stored on your device

Driftfall saves your progress and settings in the app's own storage: where you've landed, what you've collected, the marks your landings left on the ground, and the options you picked in the menu. If you delete the app, they're removed. A backup of your device includes them.

Third parties

Driftfall includes the Jolt Physics library, which runs on your device and sends nothing anywhere. Driftfall doesn't use any third-party services.

Children

Driftfall doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.
