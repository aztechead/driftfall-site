Support

Driftfall for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad

What you need

You need an iPhone with iOS 27 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 27 or later. The game plays in landscape. On iPhone Duo it fills the inner display.

Controls

The left stick is in the bottom-left corner and sets throttle and yaw. The right stick is in the bottom-right corner and turns the ship. Each one appears where your thumb lands.

Drag or pinch anywhere else to move the camera. Double-tap to recentre it, or to change the view when it's already centred.

The gear at the top opens the menu. Self-levelling, ship-relative sticks and latched throttle are under Controls.

Hold an attitude

  1. Touch and hold the button at the bottom centre. The wheel opens and the game slows.
  2. Slide your finger to a wedge.
  3. Lift your finger. The ship uses its own wheels and jets to hold that attitude.

To cancel, lift your finger in the middle of the wheel. You can't start a hold on the ground, and a hold stops at touchdown.

The display

The circle under the top-left text is the nav scope. It zooms from 3 km to 50 m as you approach, and the range shows beside the ring. The white line is where you'll be in 5 seconds. The orange X is where your path meets the ground with no more thrust.

  • PAD: distance, bearing and height of the nearest pad.
  • LANDS ... OFF PAD: how far from that pad the orange X is.
  • CRYSTAL: the nearest crystal you haven't collected.
  • PROBE: this planet's derelict probe, until you find it.

Landing limits

The legs survive these touchdown speeds:

  • Rock: up to 6 m/s.
  • Ice and crust: up to 8 m/s.
  • Dust: up to 10 m/s.

The ground under the ship shows at the top left. A cue beside the speed tapes tells you when to start your burn. Pads refuel and repair the ship.

Start a new star system

A new star system discards all your progress. To start one, open the menu and choose New star system.
