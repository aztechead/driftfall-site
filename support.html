Driftfall for iPhone

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. The game plays in landscape, and on iPhone Duo it fills the inner display.

Controls

The left stick is in the bottom-left corner and sets throttle and yaw. The right stick is in the bottom-right corner and turns the ship. Each one appears where your thumb lands.

Drag or pinch anywhere else to move the camera. Double-tap to recentre it, or to change the view when it's already centred.

The gear at the top opens the menu. Self-levelling, ship-relative sticks and latched throttle are under Controls.

Holding an attitude

Touch and hold the button at the bottom centre to open the wheel. The game slows while it's open. Slide to a wedge and let go, and the ship holds that attitude using its own wheels and jets. Let go in the middle to cancel. A hold can't start on the ground, and it turns off at touchdown.

Reading the display

The circle under the top-left text is the nav scope. It zooms from 3 km down to 50 m as you approach, with the range shown beside the ring. The white line is where you'll be in 5 seconds, and the orange X is where your path meets the ground if you stop thrusting.

  • PAD: distance, bearing and height of the nearest pad.
  • LANDS ... OFF PAD: how far from that pad the orange X is.
  • CRYSTAL: the nearest crystal you haven't collected.
  • PROBE: this planet's derelict probe, until you find it.

Landing

Legs survive up to 6 m/s on rock, 8 m/s on ice and crust, and 10 m/s on dust. The ground you're over shows at the top left, and a cue beside the speed tapes tells you when to start your burn. Pads refuel and repair the ship.

Starting over

Open the menu and choose New star system. This discards all your progress.
